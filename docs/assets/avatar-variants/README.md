# Avatar variants

These are project-avatar concepts for the 3D Station Calibration project. Each
variant is provided at 512x512 and at GitLab's recommended 192x192 upload size.

Upload `*-192.png` under **Settings > General > Naming, topics, avatar**.

| Variant | Idea |
| --- | --- |
| `nozzle-lens` | Recommended. Full camera-lens frame, three spheres on a dotted path, and a simple top-mounted cut-cone nozzle holding the sharp centre sphere. Left sphere is smaller and blurred; right sphere is larger and blurred. |
| `nozzle-clean` | Same scene without the large lens ring. A small circular lens emblem provides the camera cue, leaving more space for the spheres at small sizes. |
| `nozzle-depth` | The three spheres follow a diagonal path, making the size/depth change more pronounced. |

All variants omit the calibration board. The centre sphere has a crisp border
with no halo. The smaller left and larger right spheres use smooth, perfectly
circular soft edges and halos to show out-of-focus positions.

The top-level `3dsc-avatar.png` and `3dsc-avatar-192.png` are the recommended
`nozzle-lens` variant.
